Gelly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Gelly (“we”, “the app”) is a habit-and-focus app. We built it to respect your privacy: your data stays on your device by default, and we don’t run ad networks or sell anything about you.

What we collect

Habit data — the habits you create, your sessions, streaks, minutes, and settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not upload it to our servers (we don’t operate one for your habit data).

Sign in with Apple — if you sign in, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, on first sign-in, optionally your name. We store this on your device to personalize the app. We never receive your Apple ID password or email unless you choose to share it, and we don’t share it with anyone.

Purchases — subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information; we never see or store your card or billing details. We only receive from Apple whether you currently have an active subscription.

Screen Time / app blocking — when you turn on blocking, you select which apps to shield using Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) system. Those selections stay on your device and are managed by iOS; the app cannot see your usage of other apps, and this information never leaves your device.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build profiles about you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, iOS schedules a local notification on your device. No reminder data is sent to us.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time: Settings → Delete account, which permanently removes your habits, streaks, and settings from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes local data. To cancel a subscription, use Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.

Children

Gelly is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; we’ll revise the date above when we do.
